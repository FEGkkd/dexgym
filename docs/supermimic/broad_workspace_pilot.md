# Broad dual TCP workspace pilot: measured limits and next gate

This is an extension of the causal, 50 Hz arm-only SuperMimic task. The
algorithm remains masked WM8, learned-dynamics BPTT32, survival return and
TD(λ), with 14 arm actions and the unchanged 54-DoF Newton robot. The 40
finger joints stay at Home and do not enter Actor, Critic, World Model or
Replay. No inverse kinematics, target joint regression or teacher policy is
used. Forward kinematics is used to generate and score TCP goals.

## Two distinct questions

1. **Broad continuous following:** Can one policy track smooth, causal dual
   TCP SE(3) target streams from many safe regions of the joint range?
2. **Arbitrary pose arrival:** From an independently sampled safe configuration,
   can the policy reach a different, feasible dual TCP SE(3) target and hold it?

The first does not prove the second. A generated joint path is only a witness
for FK target creation and a safe physics reset. Its joint angles are not a
policy goal, reward term or action target. The far-goal bank deliberately
separates reset witnesses from discarded FK goal witnesses. Its static 21-point
interpolation screen is a kinematic feasibility check; dynamic safety is
measured in Newton.

All targets are fixed-base position and SO(3) at 50 Hz. The Actor receives the
current target, its proprioceptive observation history and its own previous
action. The 42D dynamics state is arm `q14 + qd14 + qcmd14`; finger state is
excluded. The analytic action is `qcmd_next = clamp(qcmd + 0.01 tanh(a))`,
interpolated through the existing ten Newton substeps.

The v1 bank regenerated byte-for-byte under the extended generator. Broad v2
samples starts across the joint-limit box with 0.08 rad margins. Its maximum
target-witness per-frame joint step is 0.00653 rad. The generated dual TCP
position extrema expand from the local region near Home to approximately
`[-0.213,0.947] × [-0.676,0.670] × [0.568,1.914] m` for the left arm and
`[-0.944,0.234] × [-0.688,0.634] × [0.561,1.920] m` for the right arm.
These are sampled bounds, **not** a proof that every pose inside the box is
reachable or safe.

An independent static collision-free Monte Carlo sample of 3,000 joint poses
was compared to training-bank targets at five-frame intervals using 40 nearby
position candidates. Only 39.2% / 38.4% of sampled left/right TCP positions
had a bank target within 5 cm. The approximate fractions with both position
within 5 cm and orientation within 10° were 0.067% / 0.1%. This nearest-neighbor
screen is a conservative sample-based coverage estimate, not a complete
reachability measure. Broad joint-angle extrema alone do not establish dense
SE(3) coverage. Full-workspace claims require much denser target sampling,
independent goal distributions and closed-loop Newton success measurements.
The original local iteration-820 checkpoint also completed an independent
one-environment, 200-step v1 Newton regression with no hard termination and
left/right mean TCP position error 1.00/0.65 mm. The old reference hash key
was preserved exactly for checkpoint compatibility.

## Current physical and training evidence

On 16 twelve-second broad smooth test clips with seed 9201:

| Policy | Full tracking success | Mean TCP position L/R | Hard failures |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Zero action | 0/16 | 58.9 / 51.5 mm | 0 |
| Original local iteration 820 | 0/16 | 173.2 / 136.5 mm | 3 contact terminations |
| Broad v2, iteration 400 | 0/16 | 74.1 / 69.9 mm | 0 |

The v2 strict-pure run used 4,096 Newton environments and the existing 512×3
World Model, 512 imagination starts, 32-step BPTT admission and 5×4 Critic
updates. At iteration 500 it had 8.19M real transitions, 16,000 WM updates,
40 Actor updates, 800 Critic updates and zero nonfinite optimizer events.
The strict trust gate frequently closed: deterministic 32-step TCP position
P95 was near or above its 5 mm threshold. Continuing only this configuration
for thousands more iterations is not justified by the independent physics
result. Its checkpoint is an experimental artifact, not a deployable policy.
The compact model and optimizer snapshot is
[`teleop_broad_v2_iter500_experimental.pt`](../../models/supermimic/teleop_broad_v2_iter500_experimental.pt)
(SHA256 `dde44c51dcf22bce13213ed86caec367ebdf53c490427cdec90f25af97757e1e`).
It uses the explicit `fresh_physics` checkpoint mode: Replay, physical
collector state and old trust decisions are excluded. The full 6.5 GB Replay
checkpoint and physics traces remain in the external experiment archive.

The far-goal unseen bank was independently run with zero action on 16 Newton
environments for 600 steps: no hard failure, 0/16 reach-and-hold success,
mean final left/right position error 0.437/0.426 m and orientation error
78.0°/84.6°. A separate analyzer scores dual 5 mm / 2° arrival and 25
consecutive steps with 50 Hz net joint speed at most 0.15 rad/s. It excludes
transitions after an environment terminates and reports contact failures
separately. The regular full-clip tracking success metric must not be used for
far-goal arrival claims.

The broad smooth iteration-500 policy also scored 0/16 on those same far-goal
episodes, with three contact terminations. Its mean final left/right position
error was 0.482/0.418 m; it did not improve the zero-action baseline. This
directly falsifies treating smooth tracking from a near-target reset as
arbitrary pose arrival. A separate v3 experiment therefore starts with a
fresh random Actor and Critic while transferring only compatible World Model
weights. Its first 100 iterations collected 1.64M new Newton transitions and
performed 3,200 WM updates, but the strict BPTT32 trust gate remained closed:
noisy 32-step normalized delta RMSE was 1.575, and the deterministic TCP
position P95 model error was 13.82 mm. Actor update count remained zero.
Further training is conditioned on reducing those measured errors, not on
declaring the transfer Smoke a learned controller.
After another 100 iterations with the same 512×3 model, the noisy 32-step
normalized error was 1.573 and deterministic TCP position P95 was 12.46 mm;
Actor and Critic still had zero updates. A controlled alternative trained a
fresh 1024×3 model with 64 WM updates per iteration and 1,024 validation
starts. Its first 100 iterations also failed 32-step trust and had zero Actor
updates. The measured noisy 32-step normalized delta error was 1.768, and
deterministic 32-step TCP position P95 error was 13.49 mm. It collected 1.64M
real transitions, made 6,400 WM updates, and used 7.7 GB peak PyTorch VRAM.
Increasing width and update count alone did not resolve the failure; this
configuration was stopped rather than run for thousands of rounds.

## Reproduction

Run from this checkout with the pinned Isaac Sim 6/Newton runtime:

```bash
export PYTHONPATH=/home/ubuntu/dexgym_data/marvin_supermimic_m42/runtime_overrides:source
ISAAC_PYTHON=/home/ubuntu/isaacsim-6.0.1/bin/python
BANK=source/dexgym/assets/supermimic/references/teleop_workspace_broad_12s_seed20261107.npz

$ISAAC_PYTHON scripts/supermimic/isaac_runtime.py train_continuous_se3 \
  --config configs/supermimic/teleop_workspace_4096_broad12s_v2.json \
  --reference-bank "$BANK" --num-envs 4096 --curriculum-level 0 \
  --iterations 100 --output /tmp/dexgym_broad_stage1

$ISAAC_PYTHON scripts/supermimic/isaac_runtime.py evaluate_continuous_se3 \
  --task workspace --reference-bank "$BANK" --baseline zero \
  --num-envs 16 --seed 9201 --output /tmp/dexgym_broad_zero

$ISAAC_PYTHON scripts/supermimic/isaac_runtime.py evaluate_continuous_se3 \
  --task workspace --allow-new-reference \
  --reference-bank source/dexgym/assets/supermimic/references/teleop_workspace_far_goal_test_seed20261109.npz \
  --checkpoint /path/to/compatible/weights.pt --num-envs 16 --seed 9301 \
  --output /tmp/dexgym_far_goal_eval

$ISAAC_PYTHON scripts/supermimic/analyze_far_goal_trace.py \
  --trace /tmp/dexgym_far_goal_eval/physics_trace.pt \
  --reference-bank source/dexgym/assets/supermimic/references/teleop_workspace_far_goal_test_seed20261109.npz \
  --output /tmp/dexgym_far_goal_eval/reach_report.json

$ISAAC_PYTHON scripts/supermimic/isaac_runtime.py train_continuous_se3 \
  --config configs/supermimic/teleop_workspace_4096_far_reach12s_v3.json \
  --reference-bank source/dexgym/assets/supermimic/references/teleop_workspace_far_goal_train_seed20261111.npz \
  --num-envs 4096 --curriculum-level 0 --iterations 100 \
  --init-dynamics-from /path/to/compatible/fresh_physics.pt \
  --output /tmp/dexgym_far_goal_stage1
```

`--init-from` on the training entrypoint explicitly transfers compatible
Actor, World Model, Critic and normalizers to a new target bank. It starts
fresh Replay, optimizers, counts and Model Trust, and logs the source model.
It is model-based transfer, not a random-Actor strict-pure run. Strict Replay
resume still requires the exact original bank and training contract.
For a new random Actor, `--init-dynamics-from` copies only the compatible
World Model and its running statistics; Actor and Critic stay freshly
initialized. The 32-environment five-iteration Newton integration check
collected 640 real transitions, performed 160 WM updates and had zero hard
terminations. It did not yet establish policy learning.

## Acceptance still open

The complete SuperMimic unit suite passed **229 tests** in the pinned runtime.
This establishes the new reference, checkpoint and analysis code paths; it
does not establish task-level learning or broad reachable-pose coverage.

- Smooth broad tracking: independent 12-second Newton success on held-out
  targets from several safe joint regions, with both TCP position and SO(3).
- Arbitrary pose arrival: independent start/goal pair success and 25-step
  hold across near, medium and wide difficulty, without contact or joint
  violations.
- Dense coverage: stratified position, orientation, joint region and target
  distance measurements beyond the present finite bank. Human target streams,
  online teleoperation latency and real robot safety remain untested.

Do not label this pilot as a full-workspace controller until these gates pass.
