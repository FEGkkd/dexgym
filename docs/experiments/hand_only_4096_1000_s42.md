# Right-hand PPO scale run: 4096 environments, 1000 iterations

**Run date:** 2026-11-08. **Task commit:** `9734767`. This run kept the user's
10 mm, all-five-finger, two-step success rule and the full-contact WujiHand2
asset. It did not use object state, and did not alter the GRAB sequence bank.

## Protocol

- RSL-RL PPO, seed 42, 4096 Newton MJWarp environments, 32 steps per
  environment per iteration, 1000 iterations: **131,072,000 environment
  steps**. The 20D action targets only the right hand at 30 Hz; the arms and
  left hand hold Home. The policy and value MLPs each use two 256-unit layers.
- Training bank: 635 right-hand GRAB motions from subjects s1–s5. Held-out
  bank: 162 motions from s8. Goals advance by four 30 Hz frames after two
  consecutive steps with every fingertip inside 10 mm.
- Command: `isaaclab train --rl_library rsl_rl --task
  DexGym-Wuji-Right-Hand-Tracking-Direct-v0 --num_envs 4096
  --max_iterations 1000 --seed 42 --run_name handonly_4096_1000_s42
  physics=newton_mjwarp` with `PYTHONPATH=source` and the host's clean native
  library overlay. The original `.venv` still has unrelated damaged native
  libraries, so the overlay is needed on this host.
- Training exited 0 after 1162.22 s (19.4 min). Measured GPU use during the
  run was about 9,878 MiB on an RTX 5090. The complete local run, including 201
  checkpoints and TensorBoard events, is at
  `/home/ubuntu/dexgym/logs/rsl_rl/dexgym_wuji_right_hand_tracking/2026-11-08_01-23-27_handonly_4096_1000_s42/`
  (446 MiB, ignored by Git).

## Training and held-out results

The reported training mean episode reward rose from 6.38 at iteration 0 to
14.96 at iteration 999, and the reported mean fingertip error fell from
37.2 to 22.2 mm. Most of that change occurred in the first 200 iterations.
The reported 10 mm goal reach fraction remained close to zero. Gaussian
action standard deviation rose from 0.30 to 3.35; actions are clipped to
[-1, 1] before simulation. Reward improvement therefore does not establish
that the sequence-following objective was solved.

Held-out evaluation used deterministic inference on s8, with two independent
evaluation seeds. Each seed ran 256 environments for 1000 steps (512,000
environment steps per row across both seeds). The metric counts a subgoal only
when the actual +10 reached-goal bonus is paid. All 162 held-out motions were
encountered in each evaluated policy rollout.

| Controller | Mean tip error | 10 mm subgoals, two seeds | Completed motions | Action RMS |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Zero action | 20.61 mm | 28 | 0 | 0.000 |
| PPO iteration 500 | 19.77 mm | **47** | 0 | 0.473 |
| PPO iteration 999 | **19.04 mm** | 29 | 0 | 0.854 |

The final policy reduced average error by about 1.57 mm versus zero action,
but produced almost the same number of strict subgoals (29 vs 28). Iteration
500 had the highest strict subgoal count among the evaluated checkpoints and
still completed no sequence. A single-seed privileged joint-target replay
reached 16 subgoals in 256,000 steps with 19.83 mm mean error and no completed
motion. The final policy also completed no motion on a separate training-split
rollout (256 environments × 1000 steps, 19.37 mm mean tip error, 8 subgoals).

These results support **coarse fingertip-error learning, not successful 10 mm
consecutive tracking**. The growing action scale and loss of strict subgoals
between iterations 500 and 999 warrant a targeted control/exploration study;
they do not by themselves prove the cause. The 10 mm success definition stays
fixed. A next experiment could test a lower PPO action standard deviation,
stronger action-change regularization, or actuator/retargeting calibration,
then compare under the same held-out protocol.
